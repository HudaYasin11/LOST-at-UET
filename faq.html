FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your ultimate guide to UET Lahore

Q What is LOST@UET?
▼
LOST@UET is a gamified campus exploration app designed to help you discover the University of Engineering & Technology, Lahore

Explore 50 locations, scan QR codes, unlock discoveries, earn XP, complete quests, and become a true UET Explorer!
Q Who created LOST@UET?
▼
LOST@UET was created by two passionate CS students:

👩‍💻 Huda Yasin & Hamda Sami

🎓 Both are from the CS '25 batch at UET Lahore.

💡 This project was built as a fun side project to help students explore and navigate campus in a fun and interactive way!
Q How do I start exploring?
▼
It's super easy! 🚀

1️⃣ Create an account or Log in
2️⃣ Head to the Map page
3️⃣ Walk to a location on campus
4️⃣ Scan the QR code at that location
5️⃣ Location unlocked! You earn XP!

💡 Pro Tip: Start with locations near you and work your way across campus!
Q Is there Wi-Fi on campus?
▼
Yes! UET Lahore has eduroam available across the entire campus.

📶 You can access it once you receive your university credentials (student ID and password).

💡 Tip: If you're having trouble connecting, visit the IT help desk for assistance.
Q How do I get to UET?
▼
Bus Schedule: You can access the official bus schedule through the UET official website.

Orange Line Metro: The Sultanpura and UET Orange Line stations are right at the university's doorstep!

💡 Tip: The Orange Line is the fastest and most convenient way to reach campus.
Q Where is the library and what are its timings?
▼
📚 The UET Central Library is located near the enterance.

🕐 Timings:
  • Monday to Friday: 08:00 AM – 04:00 PM
  • Friday Prayer Break: 12:30 PM – 02:00 PM
  • Reading Hall (Mon-Fri): 04:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Reading Hall (Exam Week): 04:00 PM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)
  • Saturday & Sunday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
💡 Pro Tip: During exam season, the reading hall stays open until midnight!
Q Where are the prayer facilities on campus?
▼
There are three main prayer spots on campus:

Jamia Masjid UET — The main mosque located near the academic block (for everyone)
Library Prayer Room — A quiet prayer space inside the library
GSSC (Girls) — Top floor prayer area dedicated for female students

💡 Tip: All prayer areas are well-maintained and easily accessible.
Q How many cafes does UET have?
▼
🍽️ UET has 5 main cafeterias and cafés spread across campus:

SSC (Student Service Center) — Male Cafeteria
GSSC (Girls Student Service Center) — Girls dining area
Anexi Café (Annexee) — Famous for legendary milkshakes!
Syalani Cafe — Tea, shakes, and quick drinks
Bhola Cafe — Desi food, chai, and hangout vibes

💡 Pro Tip: Anexi's milkshakes are a must-try for every UET student!
Q Are there events at UET?
▼
Yes! UET is full of events all year round!

Societies organize their own events, workshops, and competitions
Cultural Day — Celebrate the diversity of Pakistan
Sports Week — Inter-department competitions
Welcome Party — For new students every year

💡 Tip: Follow society social media pages to stay updated!
Q Is 3 hours of sleep enough before an exam?
▼
Absolutely! If your goal is to hallucinate during the exam and forget your own name, 3 hours is the sweet spot.

Real talk: 3 hours is the classic UET student delusion of "I'll just review one more slide/chapter." so you'll probably be fine..

💡 Pro Tip: Sleep is not a suggestion. It's a prerequisite. But you'll ignore this anyway, so good luck.
Q Why is the library always full during exams?
▼
Because nothing says "academic dedication" like 200 students who haven't opened a book all semester suddenly discovering the library exists.

💡 Pro Tip: The secret is to stand in line at 7am with the other hunderds of people waiting to rush in as soon as the doors open. (Or just accept your fate and study in the cafeteria like a true procrastinator.)
Q Is attendance really mandatory?
▼
Mandatory? No. Heavily guilt-tripped and occasionally punished? Yes.

You'll hear "75% attendance is required" roughly 400 times. You'll nod. You'll skip anyway. Then you'll beg the professor for mercy at the end of the semester. It's a rite of passage.

💡 Pro Tip: Show up enough to be remembered as "that student who exists" and you'll be fine.
Q How do I make friends in uni?
▼
Step 1: Sit next to someone in class.
Step 2: Ask them for a pen.
Step 3: Congratulations, you now have a study group, a therapist, and someone to share notes with.

Or just join a society. UET has a lot of them of them. Pick one. Any one. You'll either find your people or will do free labour for them :)
Q Will I get a job after graduation?
▼
Statistically? Yes. Emotionally? You'll spend six months sending resumes into the void and wondering why you chose this degree.

But hey, UET has a decent reputation. You'll be fine. Probably. Maybe. The job market is a mystery, but so is the UET grading system.

💡 Pro Tip: Network. Cry. Network again. Repeat.
Q Is it weird to eat alone in the cafeteria?
▼
Not at all. Everyone's too busy pretending to fit in anyways.

Besides, eating alone is the only time you'll get peace and quiet on this campus. Embrace it.

💡 Pro Tip: Bring a book. Or just stare into the distance dramatically. Either works.
Q Why do seniors say 'first year is easy'?
▼
Because they've blocked out the trauma. Selective memory is a survival mechanism.

First year isn't easy, it's just that the feeling is new and exciting. Every new semester feels like "this is the hardest semester ever" but eventually there comes another one. Honestly, its a never ending loop.
Q Is it normal to have no idea what's going on in labs?
▼
Normal? It's practically the curriculum.

You'll nod along, pretend to understand the material, write a few lines, stare into the void, ask the teacher some stupid questions, constantly check the time, and wait for some hail mary moment to come and save you.

Q Why does the semester feel longest in the last two weeks?
▼
Because time is a cruel joke and the universe wants to watch you suffer through every last assignment, quiz, and project submission that you definitely should have started earlier.

Those final two weeks contain approximately 47 deadlines, 3 breakdowns, and 1 existential crisis. It's not a bug, it's a feature of the UET experience.

💡 Pro Tip: Sleep is optional. Sanity is optional. Passing is not. Good luck.
