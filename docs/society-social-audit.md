# UET Lahore Main Campus — society social-link audit

Checked 10 September 2026 (Asia/Karachi).

The supplied pasted text contained source labels rather than clickable URLs. This audit independently located pages and compared their names, campus/department descriptions, activities, and outgoing links with [UET's 42-society list for 2025–2026](https://www.uet.edu.pk/facilties/core-facilities/societies-clubs/). AWS is the additional 43rd community.

**Result:** 29 entries have an identifiable society/community page or dedicated departmental page; 3 have partial leads needing confirmation; 11 have no reliable dedicated page located in this search. This does not mean those 11 have no accounts.

**Verification limits:** A society-named LinkedIn profile with matching campus content establishes a strong identity match, not proof that UET has approved that specific account. University-hosted pages and links published by the society provide stronger attribution. Some results were readable through the search index but not through direct page opening. Instagram destinations were cross-linked by the sources below; direct Instagram inspection was generally unavailable. No account ownership was confirmed by contacting members.

## Corrections and cautions from the evidence

- The earlier pasted missing list was too broad: SAE, EHS, GYM, Justuju, Mining Engineers, UETMUN, IET, X-News, AKS, and Physica have identifiable pages. Debating and Chemical Society also yielded partial leads.
- UET's list spells the transportation chapter **ITF**, while [ITE Pakistan's student chapter directory](https://ite-pakistan.com/student-chapters/) identifies **ITE UET Lahore**. This may be a naming error, but equivalence is not proven. Confirm with the Transportation Department before assigning the ITE account to the ITF card.
- The [ASME parent-site report](https://www.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/resourcefiles/aboutasme/honors%20awards/diversity%20action%20grant/uet-lahore-final-report.pdf) is a **2015** report despite the search engine showing a newer crawl/publication label. Its historical asmeuet.com mention does not verify a current website.
- The LinkedIn page at `/company/uet-lahore` calls itself an alumni page. It should not be treated as the university's official account merely because its name is UET Lahore.
- Logos were not collected in this audit. These source pages can help locate official artwork later, but no logo has been marked verified or downloaded.

The app's society data was not changed during this verification pass.
